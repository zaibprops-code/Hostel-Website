# 📸 Upload your hostel photos here

This is the folder for **real photos** of Riwaq Hostels. Anything placed here
can be shown on the website.

## How to upload (from your phone or computer)

1. Open this folder on GitHub.
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
3. Drag in your photo(s).
4. **Commit** to the `main` branch.
5. Tell Claude "I uploaded photos" — Claude will place them on the site,
   optimise them, and add proper captions/alt text.

## Naming tips (helpful, not required)

Use simple, lowercase names with no spaces, so it's clear what each photo is:

- `lounge.jpg` — the shared sitting area / common room
- `room-single.jpg`, `room-double.jpg` — bedrooms
- `washroom.jpg`, `kitchen.jpg`, `entrance.jpg`, `study.jpg`
- `exterior.jpg` — the building from outside

## Good photo tips

- **Landscape (wide)** shots work best for banners and the gallery.
- Bright, tidy, daytime photos convert best — open the curtains, make the beds.
- JPG or PNG is fine. Phone photos are perfectly good.

Once your photos are here, Claude wires them into the homepage, gallery,
room pages and branch page.
