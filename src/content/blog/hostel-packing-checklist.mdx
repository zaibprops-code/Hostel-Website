Packing for hostel life is an art: bring enough to be comfortable, not so much
that you can't fit it in your room. Here's a field-tested list.

## Bedding & comfort

- Bedsheets, a pillow and a pillowcase
- A light blanket or duvet (and a warmer one for winter)
- A small towel and a bath towel

Most hostels — Riwaq included — provide the **bed, mattress and storage**, so
you're bringing the soft layer on top.

## Daily essentials

- Toiletries and a wash bag
- A padlock for your personal storage
- A reusable water bottle
- A power extension board (you'll always want more sockets)

## Study & work

- Laptop and **all** its chargers
- A pair of headphones for shared spaces
- Notebooks, pens, and any course materials

## The things people forget

- **Slippers / sandals** for the room and washroom
- **A few hangers**
- **Basic medicines** — paracetamol, plasters, anything you take regularly
- **Copies of your documents** (CNIC, student ID) for registration
- **A small first-week snack stash**

## What you *don't* need to bring

If your hostel provides furniture, WiFi, hot water and housekeeping, leave the
bulky stuff at home. Check what's included first — see our
[admission checklist](/parents#admission) for exactly what to bring to Riwaq.

Travel light, settle in fast.
